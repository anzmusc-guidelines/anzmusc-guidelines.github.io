## PDF status (21 November 2026)

**Publisher PDFs could not be downloaded from this cloud session.** Publisher websites are outside the session's network policy; PubMed Central's website serves its PDFs only behind a CAPTCHA, which I did not attempt to bypass; Europe PMC's PDF endpoint sits behind a bot-check; and NCBI retired its open-access download API in August 2026.

**What was produced instead:** for the 12 papers with a full-text record in PubMed Central, the complete article text was retrieved through the PubMed connector and rendered to a clean A4 PDF. Each file is named `...-PMCtext.pdf` and carries a header stating that it is a text rendering of the PMC record, not the publisher's typeset PDF (figures, tables and reference lists may be missing or reformatted), with the DOI and PMC links. They are in `.claude/drafts/pdfs/` and in the zip file sent with this note. They are not committed to the repository.
